Normal is quiet — no shape, no alarm. The three ALARM levels each own a silhouette (dot → triangle → octagon) and a fixed left-to-right rank. Strip the hue entirely and they stay unambiguous: presence-of-shape means something is wrong, and the silhouette escalates with severity. That is the validation test — if it reads in greyscale, it reads for every form of color vision deficiency.

Redundantly coded — never hue alone. Normal is quiet (no shape). The three alarm levels each carry a distinct silhouette (dot → triangle → octagon), a text label, and a fixed rank, so criticality survives deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia, and greyscale printing. Hues are tuned from the IEC-60601 monitor convention (blue advisory → amber warning → vermilion critical) but shape does the real work.
